# TOFU Tactical How-To / System

Write a **tactical how-to top-of-funnel post in your own voice**: the archetype where you hand over a concrete, numbered, runnable setup — not a concept to understand, a system to copy. Every step must be something the reader can execute in their next work session, with real settings/paths/prices, not generic advice.

The goal of every post: a stranger copies the exact setup into their own workflow before they finish reading.

---

## Classification — is this actually a Tactical How-To?

Test: could a reader open a new tab and DO step 1 right now, with the exact information in the post (a URL, a setting name, a price, an exact click-path)? If yes for every step, it's this archetype.

**Not this archetype if:**
- The "steps" are just a list of external links/resources → Resource Roundup (`tofu-resource-list`).
- The numbered items decode a concept's parts rather than actions to take → Conceptual Explainer (`tofu-explainer`).
- The numbered steps exist only to prove a belief wrong → Contrarian (`tofu-contrarian`); check whether the post's real point is "here's a system" (How-To) vs "you're wrong about X" (Contrarian).

---

```
[HOOK — 2 lines: the shallow way, named, then rejected]

[1 line: the shallow way — what they're missing]

[credibility beat — see <BRAND_DIR>/linkedin-voice.md]

1️⃣ Step 1: [concrete action]
[exact detail — name, path, setting, price]

2️⃣ Step 2: [concrete action]
[exact detail]

... (repeat, 3-7 steps, each with its own exact detail)

[closer — ties the system to an outcome]

(or)

[engagement question]

----

```

## Signature voice moves (archetype-specific)

- **The shallow-use contrast hook.** "Most people use X like [demeaning comparison]." — vending machine, spell checker, a search bar. For hook variety beyond this signature move, pull from `references/hook-template-bank.md` (8 templatized hook patterns from the golden set — shallow-use callout, priced-plan downgrade, if-I-had-to, problem-then-count, elite-minority stat gap, bounded-time promise, tool-can-now reframe, plain how-to authority); match the template to the setup you have rather than forcing the shallow-use hook on a foil-less topic.
- **Hook line 2 carries a concrete number, timeframe, or named foil** (PROMOTED lever, Round 3). Whatever hook pattern you use, make line 2 quantify the payoff, not just tease it: "6 prompts turn it into a strategist," "60 minutes on it beats another week of posts," "7 habits get 3x from the same $20 plan," "4 weekend layers turn it into a full team." A line-2 that names a count/timeframe/foil out-scored a soft "here's a better way" on every scored round. Still obey L1 ≤ 62 / L2 ≤ 50.
- **"There's a completely different way to use it."** — the pivot line into the system.
- **Exact concrete detail on every step.** A real price ($20/month), a real path (`.claude/CLAUDE.md`), a real setting name (Extended Thinking), never "optimize your prompts."
- **The outcome-tied closer.** "X doesn't know what good looks like until you tell it." / "Build the system first. The outputs follow."

## Judge this archetype by ITS success metric: SAVES and IMPLEMENTATION, not comments

A how-to wins when readers save it to actually do later, or reply saying they just tried step 1. It is judged on genuine executability, not argument-starting or reshare-worthiness. If a draft's steps are too vague to literally execute, it fails on its own terms even if the prose reads well.

---

## Length target
1,600-2,200 characters. Every step needs its exact detail — a step with only a verb and no concrete specific ("optimize your workflow") is under-built. If short, add the missing exact detail to a thin step before padding elsewhere.

**Land at 6-7 executable steps, not 5** (PROMOTED lever, Round 3). When the source system has more than 7 items, do not compress to 5 — restore the highest-value concrete step (a real setting, path, prompt, or named section) up to 6-7, each carrying its own specific. Pairing a sharpened hook with one restored concrete step per thin draft is what moved the pairwise save/implementation vote in scoring; a 5-step draft left executability on the table. Never exceed 7 (the reader won't do more).

## What NOT to do
- ❌ A step that's advice, not an action ("think about your prompts" instead of "reset context every 15 turns").
- ❌ More than 7 steps (loses executability; the reader won't do 10 things).
- ❌ Steps that are actually external resource links → that's a Resource Roundup.
- ❌ AI-tell vocabulary, unicode-bold in the hook — see `<BRAND_DIR>/linkedin-voice.md`.

## Output Format
Follow `<BRAND_DIR>/linkedin-voice.md`'s mandatory anti-AI-pass lint step before presenting any draft.

**Draft → check → rewrite loop (mandatory, max 3 loops).** Write the draft, then run
it through `post-grader`'s ordered procedure (hook checks including the
count-vs-outcome sub-check, body checks, family-specific structure checks) rather
than an informal read — that ordering exists because informal checks have missed
real issues before. Rewrite based on every failed/gamble check the grader surfaces.
Repeat the grader pass on the rewrite. Stop as soon as every check passes; stop at 3
loops regardless and name what's still failing if it hasn't converged. Output only
the final version — the grading itself stays internal, not shown to the user unless
they ask to see it.
