**Structural breakdown: why this is the canonical roundup:**

| Beat | What it does | The rule it encodes |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Institution (Anthropic) + price ($10,000) + free in one line | Borrow authority in line 1; the price anchors the value |
| Sweetener | "13 courses. Certificates included. No catch." | Answers the three instant objections (how much? proof? catch?) in 7 words |
| Tease | "organized by who you actually are" | The grouping is announced as the value. The reader is promised a filter, not a dump |
| Group headers | IF YOU'RE NEW TO AI / DEVELOPER OR BUILDER / EDUCATION OR NONPROFITS | The who-it's-for filter operates at the SECTION level, so even a skimmer finds their track |
| Items | Name + one line (what it is or what it enables) + link | No bare links; no item justifies itself with adjectives, only with use |
| Frame line | "All self-paced. All free. Certificates straight from Anthropic." | The three-beat compression; the institution name closes the trust loop the hook opened |
| Reframe | "Most people are still Googling… / The people who finish even one of these aren't." | The two-line divide that gives the resharer a reason to look smart. This is the line people quote when they repost |
| Question | "Which track fits where you are right now?" | References the grouping back; low-key; the post doesn't need comments to win |
| Footer | Repost ask phrased as protecting the reader's network | The CTA matches the metric: shares, not comments |

**Why this is the strongest roundup reference:** share/like 0.214, the highest of
your own roundups in the golden set, and above the archetype median of 0.14. Note
what it does NOT have: no sponsor block, no comment-gate, no humor beat beyond the dry
"No catch." The list and the frame do all the work.

**On recycling a dead list: same post, reposted verbatim months later.** Result: 57 likes, 55
comments, **0 shares**. Same body, byte-for-byte identical, only the sponsor PS block differs (this repost pitched a different
free workshop). The event the hook borrows ("Anthropic just made... free") was true the
first time and stale the second; a roundup's authority decays with the freshness of the
event it announces. Refresh the substance or pick a new list.

| Beat | High performer | This post |
|---|---|---|

a comment-gate and a self-promotional PS instead of one clean share-oriented ask. Good
list structure alone doesn't guarantee reshare virality if the CTA is fighting itself.

**What this adds:** low performer 3 is the cleanest example of the archetype's most basic
failure mode — a hook that manufactures hype instead of borrowing real authority. Compare
its "1000x your builder skills" against the high performer's literal, checkable
"$10,000... 13 courses... certificates included." Nobody reshares an unverifiable promise
from a stranger; everybody can vouch for a name they recognize.
