## Contrarian ⭐ — "An Anthropic engineer just told us not to build agents" · [2,734 likes · 335 shares · 324 comments]

**[HOOK]**
> An Anthropic engineer just told us not to build agents.
> Barry Zhang runs agent infrastructure at Anthropic.

**[BRIDGE]** — the angle + a light credibility beat
> He just gave a 14-minute talk on what they've actually learned.
> And the whole thing reads like a polite "please stop."
> I've been building my own AI tools for months now.
> And almost every assumption I started with got corrected.
> Here's the breakdown. Save it before you start your next build:

**[MEAT]** — 1️⃣ + ↳, concrete
> 1️⃣ Don't build an agent. Build a workflow.
> ↳ If you can map the decision tree, a workflow wins every time.
> ↳ Agents are for ambiguous, high-value tasks only.
> 2️⃣ Keep it embarrassingly simple.
> ↳ An agent is 3 things: environment + tools + system prompt.
> ↳ Any complexity up front kills your iteration speed.
> 3️⃣ Think like your agent, not like yourself.
> ↳ Your agent sees 10–20K tokens, not your whole context.
> ↳ Paste your prompt back into Claude. Ask what's ambiguous. Fix that.

**[MIC DROP]** — your contrarian reframe + whole-talk-in-one-sentence
> Most builders don't have an agent problem.
> They have a workflow problem dressed up as an agent.
> Autonomy isn't the goal. Reliability is.
> The teams shipping working AI in 2026 won't be the ones with the cleverest architecture…
> They'll be the ones who picked the boring one and shipped twice as fast.
> That's the whole talk in one sentence.

**[QUESTION]**
> What's the one thing you're building right now that's secretly a workflow?
> Drop a comment below.

**[CTA]**
> ♻️ Repost to help builders in your network ship working AI.
> And follow [your name] for more on building with Claude.

**Signature moves:** "[Name/role] just [did X]" + who-they-are line → the angle ("a polite
'please stop'") → "Here's the breakdown. Save it before…" → the reframe "Most people don't
have an X problem. They have a Y problem dressed up as an X" → the whole-thing-in-one-sentence
mic drop.

**Why this is the strongest contrarian reference:** cmt/like ratio 0.118 (above the 0.11
archetype median), and the belief being corrected ("agents are the goal") is stated,
attacked with a concrete symptom, and resolved with a two-line reframe — the tightest
version of the skeleton in the golden dataset.
