# TOFU Relatable Mirror

Write a **relatable-mirror top-of-funnel post in your own voice**: the archetype where you name a universal feeling, belief, struggle, or aspiration everyone quietly recognizes, and reflects it back so the reader thinks "that's me" — then reframes it from limitation into possibility. This is an **emotional-resonance** post about work, life, mindset, identity, leadership, or career. It is NOT a concept decode, NOT a runnable system, NOT an argument that you're wrong.

The goal of every post: a stranger reads the hook, feels caught mid-thought, and either comments "this is exactly me right now" or shares/tags someone it describes.

---

## Classification — is this actually a Relatable Mirror?

Test: does the post name a feeling, belief, or life-truth the reader already carries (not a concept they need taught, not a task they need to run), and reflect it back so they feel SEEN? If a stranger's honest reaction is "how did they know" or "I needed this today" rather than "oh THAT'S what that means" or "I'll copy this setup," it's this archetype.

**Not this archetype if:**
- One sticky metaphor decodes a concept into named layers → Conceptual Explainer (`tofu-explainer`).
- The numbered items are executable actions with real settings/paths/prices the reader runs today → Tactical How-To (`tofu-howto`). **This is the most common misfile — see the hard line below.**
- The payload is external links/resources → Resource Roundup (`tofu-resource-list`).
- The post's real point is "you believe X and X is wrong" delivered as an argument, not a validation → Contrarian (`tofu-contrarian`). A mirror can open by naming a wrong belief, but it then *empathizes and reframes*; a contrarian *prosecutes*.
- It's built around a real image/graphic as the payload → Meme (`tofu-meme`).
- It breaks down a specific third-party talk/launch/announcement → News Breakdown (`tofu-news-breakdown`).

**The hard line vs How-To (numbered items are the trap):** many mirror posts contain a numbered list (your own #1 has 5; Luke Tobin's "10 ways," Charlie Lass's "7 ways," Noemi Kis's "5 things"). The list does NOT make it a how-to. Ask of each item: is it an **executable action** with a concrete specific ("reset context every 15 turns," "go to claude.com/download, pay the $20") → How-To; or is it a **mindset shift / realization / principle** ("Stop thinking not knowing is permanent," "Separate facts from emotion," "Boundaries are survival tools") → Relatable Mirror. Mirror-list items are things you *believe or notice differently*, not things you *do in your next work session*.

## The two shapes (both are golden, pick by the material)

This archetype has two proven shapes in the data. Choose by what you actually have.

### Shape A — Short Reframe (punch)
A universal truth stated, then flipped, in a handful of lines. No list, no story. Wins on sheer shareability of the reframe itself.
```
[HOOK — 2 lines: the common belief/situation, then the flip]
Top talent doesn't just leave for more money.
They leave for less B.S.

[2-4 lines: the reframe expanded, still tight]
You can't out-hire a toxic culture.
Fix what's broken before you scale what's not.

[1 line: the mic-drop restatement]
If your bucket is leaking, pouring more water in won't fix the hole.

----
♻️ Repost to [inspire someone / help your network]
➕ Follow me ([your name]) for more
```
Real reference examples (Short Reframe): Luke Tobin — "Top talent doesn't just leave for more money. They leave for less B.S." (1,366 likes) · Charlie Lass — "Influence means very little. Impact means a lot. Which do you really want?" (4,757 likes) · Luke Tobin — "Most people are chasing the wrong luxuries. And it's costing them the ones that matter most." (4,686 likes).

### Shape B — Story-or-List Mirror (long)
A universal opening, an optional personal-vulnerability beat, then either a numbered set of REFLECTIONS or a lived story, closing on an affirmation + a question.
```
[HOOK — 2 lines: the universal belief/feeling, named as the reader says it]
"I don't know how to do it"
That single belief has kept more people small than their circumstances ever could.

[optional personal beat — TRUE to your real life]
Here's what I've learned about turning "I don't know" into "I'm learning":

[the mirror body — numbered REFLECTIONS or a lived story]
1️⃣ [mindset shift, not an action]
→ [the reframe]
→ [the reframe, restated concretely]

2️⃣ ... (repeat, 3-10 reflections; each a belief-shift, never a runnable step)

[the turn — the single line the whole post exists to deliver]
The only difference between you and the expert? They started.

[affirming closer + engagement question]
What's one thing you've been putting off because of not knowing?

----
♻️ Repost [for someone who needs this today]
➕ Follow me ([your name]) for more
```
Real reference examples (Story-or-List): your own — "\"I don't know how to do it\"" + 5 mindset reflections (the MINE row, this archetype's anchor) · Stephanie Hills — "At 52, I had to start over. At 61, I know why." + a life timeline + 5 lessons (2,327 likes) · Will McTighe — "Success isn't from knowing everything. It's from admitting you don't." + 3 reflections (4,025 likes) · Noemi Kis — "Four simple rules that solve 90% of problems:" + reframed principles (3,630 likes).

## Signature voice moves (archetype-specific)

- **The hook is the mirror.** It names something the reader already feels. Default families: a **quoted limiting belief** ("I don't know how to do it"), a **ratio/split** ("99% try to change their life. Only 1% try to change their mind."), a **flat reframe couplet** ("Success isn't from knowing everything. It's from admitting you don't."). For hook variety pull a proven template from `references/hook-template-bank.md` — 8 fixed-word mechanisms with slot tests and real examples from the 20-row golden set. Match the mechanism to the truth you actually have; never copy a hook verbatim.
- **The reframe is the payload.** Every mirror post turns a limitation into a possibility: "I don't know how" → "I'm learning"; a messy career → an advantage; not knowing → where every expert started. If a draft names a feeling but never flips it, it's a diary entry, not a mirror.
- **Numbered items are REFLECTIONS, not actions.** Mindset shifts, realizations, principles — "Stop catastrophising," "Boundaries are survival tools," "Separate facts from emotion." The reader nods, they don't execute. (See the hard line vs How-To above.)
- **Optional personal-vulnerability beat.** Your own posts and the strongest golden rows earn the reframe with a first-person admission ("7 years ago, I was the reactive founder," "At 52, I got laid off"). Powerful, but **only if TRUE to your real life** — never invent a biography (per `<BRAND_DIR>/linkedin-voice.md` truth/authorship). If you have no real lived beat for the topic, use Shape A or a non-personal mirror.
- **Warm second person.** "You" and "your" carry this archetype — the reader must feel addressed, not lectured.
- **The affirming closer + the "what's yours?" question.** Ends on possibility, then hands the mic to the reader ("What's one thing you've been putting off?", "What's your #1 most undervalued skill?"). This is what converts resonance into comments.

**Floor-check (run before finalizing):** per `<BRAND_DIR>/hook-floor-check.md`
(cross-creator structural finding, not voice-specific) — is it concrete not
abstract, is the register right. Relatable Mirror's baseline from that analysis:

## Judge this archetype by ITS success metric: SHARES and COMMENTS, not saves

A mirror wins when readers **share it** ("this describes someone I know") or **comment** ("this is exactly me right now"). It is a *feel-seen resonance asset*, not a study reference (that's the Explainer) and not an implementation checklist (that's the How-To). In the golden set the high performers skew to strong shares/like and comments/like, not save-heavy behavior. If a draft reads as clever but nobody would tag a friend or confess in the comments, it has failed on its own terms even if the prose is clean. If it drifts into teaching a structure it's become an Explainer; if it drifts into prosecuting a belief it's become a Contrarian — pull it back to validation-and-reframe.

## Length target
No tournament has tuned this archetype, so length follows the data, and the data is bimodal:
- **Shape A (Short Reframe): 300-900 characters.** A tight flip. Do not pad it into a list to hit a word count — the punch is the point (Luke Tobin's 1,366-like and 4,686-like posts are both short).
- **Shape B (Story-or-List): 1,400-2,600 characters.** Room for the vulnerability beat and 3-10 reflections. Each reflection needs its own 2-3 line block (the shift + the reframe restated); a one-bare-line reflection is under-built.

Pick the shape that fits the material and commit to its range. Don't stretch a Short Reframe or compress a life-story.

## What NOT to do
- ❌ Numbered items that are executable actions with settings/paths/prices → that's a How-To, not a mirror.
- ❌ Inventing a personal story, age, job, or life event that isn't true to you (per `<BRAND_DIR>/linkedin-voice.md`).
- ❌ A sticky metaphor that decodes a concept into layers → that's the Explainer.
- ❌ Prosecuting a belief as wrong instead of validating-then-reframing → that's the Contrarian.
- ❌ Naming a feeling with no reframe (a diary entry, not a mirror).
- ❌ Toxic-positivity or generic affirmation with no lived specific behind it.
- ❌ AI-tell vocabulary, unicode-bold in the hook — see `<BRAND_DIR>/linkedin-voice.md`.

## Output Format
Follow `<BRAND_DIR>/linkedin-voice.md`'s mandatory anti-AI-pass lint step before presenting any draft.

**Draft → check → rewrite loop (mandatory, max 3 loops).** Write the draft, then run
it through `post-grader`'s ordered procedure (hook checks including the
count-vs-outcome sub-check, body checks, family-specific structure checks) rather
than an informal read — that ordering exists because informal checks have missed
real issues before. Rewrite based on every failed/gamble check the grader surfaces.
Repeat the grader pass on the rewrite. Stop as soon as every check passes; stop at 3
loops regardless and name what's still failing if it hasn't converged. Output only
the final version — the grading itself stays internal, not shown to the user unless
they ask to see it.
